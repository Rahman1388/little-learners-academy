## Learning design and safety gate

- Use a concrete / pictorial / symbolic teaching sequence, with a real explanation before independent practice wherever possible.
- Be explicit about why an answer is correct, not just its numeric result. Make wrong-answer feedback encouraging and actionable.
- Provide accessible mobile/tablet tap targets, clear contrast, no forced timer, optional hints and replay.
- Prioritize depth and misconception checks over inflating the activity count. Record question attempts as practice, not proof of mastery.
- Keep progress local, without accounts, identifiers, microphones, advertising or tracking. Preserve existing saved progress and previously approved pronunciation recordings.
- Keep formal Arabic, Qatar history/identity and Islamic Studies review-gated. Draft Arabic maths supports need a competent native-speaking reviewer.
- Qualified maths teacher review, Arabic proofreading, school-specific Qatar alignment, and measured child/parent usability are **outstanding**. No such approvals should be claimed.
