This introductory sorting activity reinforces common early-primary science concepts: living and non-living things, everyday materials, five senses, and what living things need. Categories are simplified teaching examples rather than exhaustive scientific classifications. Explanations accompany each correct answer. No microphone, accounts, advertising or new pronunciation recordings are used; Arabic terms are vocabulary support, not a certified Arabic science syllabus.
