For parents and teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

This hands-on Grade 1 place-value bridge develops tens and ones within 100. Representations use groups of ten and individual counters. A correct answer requires the right number of tens and ones, not just a matching total. It supplements, but does not claim certification against, any school's Qatar curriculum. No recordings or accounts are needed. Your progress stays on this device.
