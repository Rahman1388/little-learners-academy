## Bilingual brand approaches — shortlisting, not clearance

**Arabic-inspired directions**
- **NabtaNova Academy — أكاديمية نبتة نوفا.** Evokes `نبتة` (young plant / growth) and a distinctive coined second element. A quick exact-phrase public-web check did not surface an obvious matching school, **but this is not clearance**. Other Nabta-branded businesses exist (for example `https://nabtagrow.com/`).
- **Faseela Quest — فسيلة كويست.** `فسيلة` can reference a young palm shoot; playful and Gulf-relevant. Check native pronunciation, the Arabic `فَسِيلة` meaning, English spelling alternatives, local trademark, social handles and store listings.

**International-English directions**
- **BrightPebble Academy — أكاديمية برايت بيبل.** Easy visual branding in English, but `BrightPebble` appears in online name-idea lists, and the Arabic transcription is less natural. **Higher distinctiveness concern**.
- **MinoBloom Academy — أكاديمية مينو بلوم.** International pronunciation, friendly for early years, but requires collision and Arabic cultural/language vetting; public search has unrelated `minobloom` uses.

**Clear name conflicts from web checks**
- **Luma Learn** education platform already exists: `https://www.lumalearn.ai/` (learners, parents, teachers); do not presume `Luma Learn Academy` unique.
- **Learnora Kids** is already on Google Play and App Store: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rvscript.learnora`.
- **SparkNest Academy** education platform already active: `https://www.sparknestacademy.in/`.
- **CurioSprout** sells kids' learning kits: `https://curiosprout.com/`.
- **WonderSteps** appears in educational products and even a UK education-services company: `https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16592595`.

**Before selecting a brand:** separate official Qatar trademark and international marks search; compare exact and phonetic matches in Arabic and English; check Apple App Store / Google Play; domain and social-handle availability; culturally competent Arabic editing. No legal assurance is possible from casual Google searches.
