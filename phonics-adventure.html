For parents & teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

Inspired by effective early-reading activities, but this is an original supplementary activity. The letter tiles support visual word-building. Whole words are played three times with pauses so learners can hear them without an instant 0:00 cutoff. These are repeated original recordings, not newly recorded individual phoneme sounds. Isolated phoneme voiceovers and word pronunciation still need qualified teacher review.

English word audio follows your saved app voice choice. Arabic uses the clearer male recording for this demo while the female Arabic voice is reviewed. No account, adverts or tracking.
