For adults • إرشادات للأهل

Listening: An adult reads the hidden script aloud. This is guided practice, not an independent listening test or verified audio.

Speaking: A child practises with an adult. The button records an attempt, not a judgment of fluency or pronunciation. The app does not record voices.

Reading: Each original passage has one primary question plus an optional additional comprehension question. An adult can reveal a supporting sentence and discuss the evidence. The bonus question does not change saved progress.

Writing: Children build one guided sentence, then may write an original sentence using an optional starter. Adults—not the app—check the independent writing. The typed original sentence is not saved or uploaded and will disappear if the lesson is changed or refreshed. Avoid names and other personal information.

Phonics: Eight optional word-family items follow the -ing/-and, -all/-ack, -ain/-ink and -ay/-ick patterns (2026–2027 Grade 2 Semester 1 overview, p.17). Seven more optional questions introduce ch, sh, th, wh, ph and silent-e spellings (overview, p.2). All 15 are on-screen letter-recognition tasks, not spoken pronunciation assessments, and do not change saved progress.
