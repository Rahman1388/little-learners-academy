👨‍👩‍👧 How this compares internationally

International learning platforms may span several years, offer thousands of individual problems, and provide adaptive assessment. Counting small questions alone is not a meaningful comparison: 151 Grade 1 challenges do not equal 151 complete lessons or a full Grades 1–6 syllabus.

Our next quality milestone is to improve Grade 1 depth: ordered lesson sequences, explanations, review of tricky answers, and parent-readable progress. Only then should later-grade activities be released.

Learning themes are broad overlap with primary-school concepts, not an official mapping to Qatar Ministry of Education standards. Arabic-language, Islamic Studies, and Qatar-history core modules remain locked pending qualified curriculum and language review. The app is supplementary and privacy-friendly; no child account is required.
