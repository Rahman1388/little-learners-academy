💛 For parents and teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

Try about 10–15 minutes if the child is comfortable, or shorter when needed. Let children choose their favourite activity, discuss what they learned, and include breaks and offline play. You can do the sessions in any order, on any dates.

These activities support foundational Grade 1 skills and family participation; they are not an approved Qatar curriculum or a substitute for classroom teaching. Arabic labels support families while formal Arabic and Islamic Studies content remain subject to specialist review.

No child names, accounts, advertisements, microphone access, or data sharing are required.
