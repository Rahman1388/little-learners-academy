👨‍👩‍👧 Parent and teacher review • إرشادات الأهل والمعلمين

The three learning bands contain four original, bilingual questions each (12 in the draft). These are science-inspired practice prompts, not 12 certified lessons, a school assessment, or a validated national curriculum. The published app's separately audited 475 MCQs are unchanged. Visuals are original HTML/CSS and symbols; they are not wildlife photographs.

Language: All directions, answer choices, feedback, hints and family prompts are shown in English plus draft Arabic together. No audio plays in this pilot: new English or Arabic words must not be presented with synthetic, unreviewed pronunciation. Parents may model words and check existing recorded samples in the adult audio review studio. Human phonics, accents, word stress and Arabic vowel accuracy remain review gates.

Nature safety: Discuss and observe safely with a responsible adult; no child needs to visit a reserve. Never touch or disturb wild animals or nests, or enter mangroves or soft tidal mudflats. Use photos or household plant observation where appropriate.

Privacy and wellbeing: No accounts, ads, microphone, location, network API, analytic tracking or storage. Progress is in this open page only, does not alter stars, and resets on reload. No daily streaks or penalty for finishing early.

Release gate: A Grade 1–6 science educator and native Arabic educator must review age levels and all translations. Test keyboard, screen readers, iPhone Safari, Android, 320px width, mistakes/retry/finish, and accessible visuals before owner-approved publication.
