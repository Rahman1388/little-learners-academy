Our original color-coded concept map links the central number to its tens, ones and total. Color is always accompanied by words and numbers.

For parents and teachers

Use the captioned story to introduce tens and ones. Pause to count the sticks and dots, then ask a child to explain the map. Human review is required before classroom release, and children must be able to learn without color or motion.
