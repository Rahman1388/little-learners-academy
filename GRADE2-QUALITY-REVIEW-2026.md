# Grade 2 English — educator and parent quality review
_Updated 18 October 2026 — internal planning record, not a school or Ministry endorsement_

## Scope and verified progress

The public site offers two clearly labelled **supplementary Grade 2 English previews**:
- [Five-module four-skills preview](./grade2-four-skills.html): five themed modules × listening, speaking, reading, writing = **20 guided practice flags**. It also contains 15 optional written phonics checks, five optional reading follow-ups and five ephemeral original-writing prompts. Practice flags **do not** certify competence or oral accuracy.
- [Module 2 Word Family Adventure](./grade2-phonics-pathway.html): -ing/-and examples with seven adult-guided stages per family (**14 optional in-page steps**). A normal-speed **Hear** and slower **Slow** button use an available device English voice. The voice may differ by phone and is **not yet educator-approved**; no microphone or audio upload is used.

The earlier educator review covered **only three sample Grade 2 English reading activities**. The reviewer requested stronger learning-objective suitability and accessibility, as well as richer listening, speaking, reading and writing opportunities. This was helpful formative feedback—not certification of these later previews.

## How a reviewer can submit useful feedback

For each issue record: **page + module + skill + exact word/prompt + device + what happened + what should change + review result (Accept / Revise / Hold)**. Do not send identifiable child photos, names, speech recordings or student assessments to a public repository.

Suggested first human check: on an iPhone in Safari, open Word Family Adventure, choose **-ing** → See, tap **Hear ring** and **Slow**, then **Hear sing** and **Slow**. Record if each is audible and natural. Repeat for **-and**, then check that Continue moves to the next stage. A built-in phone voice is an optional *preview*, not a validated phonics voice model.
