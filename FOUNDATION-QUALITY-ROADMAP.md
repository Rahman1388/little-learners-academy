# Little Learners Academy — Quality-First Foundation Plan
**Checkpoint:** 18 October 2026 | **Status:** Internal development framework; not an official curriculum, certification, or app audit.

## Why this phase comes before new activity volume

Our goal is an excellent **mobile-first, bilingual supplementary learning experience** for primary pupils in Qatar, eventually covering Grades 1–6. A large question bank is not equivalent to deep teaching or an approved curriculum. Build a reproducible lesson system first, then expand it gradually. No subscriptions, advertising, external learner accounts or costly infrastructure are needed for the next phase.

The public Grade 1 foundation and limited Grades 2–6 subject pilots **already exist**. Preserve them. Do not rebuild from scratch, delete saved local progress, or claim a complete school syllabus.

## Approved long-term scope (owner decision, 19 October 2026)

**Primary planning target: at least 2,400 meaningful interactive practice activities** spanning Grades 1–6. The companion instructional target is **600 structured lessons**. Use an initial planning allowance of **100 lessons plus 400 activities per grade** (six grades × 100 = 600 lessons; six grades × 400 = 2,400 activities). This is a manageable long-term scope, not a deadline or promise that every grade is currently complete.

**Definition of a counted lesson:** one coherent learning objective with an explanation/model, accessible guided attempt, constructive feedback, independent or applied practice, and a review/check. **Definition of a counted activity:** a distinct age-appropriate playable practice task with a stable ID, subject/grade/skill mapping and verified learning purpose; a repeated quiz in a mixed challenge, an animation, or a cosmetic variant is not a new activity. Interactive activities and full lessons remain different units. Preserve a per-grade quality manifest and automated duplicate/count validation.

**Baseline (October 2026):** the existing independently audited inventory lists **451 distinct short multiple-choice questions** and **six separately tracked Grade 6 writing prompts**. Further games, guided phonics stages and visual models exist, but must be classified and deduplicated before counting them toward the 2,400-activity target. Do not present a misleading completion percentage or equate question counts with lessons.

**Expansion gates:** 50 quality-reviewed structured lessons → 150 → 300 → 600, while building distinct activities at an age-appropriate pace. Gate each phase on educator subject review, qualified Arabic review, clear child-facing audio, Qatar-school curriculum mapping where applicable, child safety and privacy, accessibility, iPhone/tablet usability and passing automated content/security checks. This is an independent supplementary platform; no government or school certification is implied.

## Independent regional competitor strategy (owner decision, 19 October 2026)

**Positioning:** Little Learners Academy will remain an **independent** child-focused supplementary education platform. The long-term commercial/educational ambition is to compete on quality, accessibility and local relevance with established international learning platforms, **without affiliation, endorsement, imitation of proprietary content, or unsupported superiority claims**.

**Geographic rollout sequence:** (1) **Qatar first**: verify bilingual foundational lessons and child safety with appropriate local educators and parents; (2) **Saudi Arabia**: adapt subject terminology, age/grade pathway, curriculum sources, cultural examples, legal/privacy rules and local Arabic usage with qualified local reviewers; (3) **United Arab Emirates**: separately map lessons to intended school systems and language needs and obtain UAE-specific teacher, accessibility, licensing and privacy reviews. **No school or Ministry recognition is presumed.**

**Common product foundation:** Preserve one maintainable core for visual maths, phonics, assessment feedback, parent guidance, accessibility and offline-friendly design. Adapt region-specific curricula only through reviewed metadata and lesson variants. Do not assume a single GCC curriculum or a universal grade-equivalence mapping.

**Product target remains 600 structured lessons and 2,400+ meaningful interactive activities overall** across Grades 1–6; do not silently triple these totals for the three countries. Regional translations/localisations must not inflate the count of genuinely distinct educational activities.

**Evidence-led competitive benchmarks:** Independent educator acceptance per objective, natural English/Arabic copy, real iPhone/Android/VoiceOver tests, learning effectiveness in supervised pilots, reliability, privacy, accessibility and family satisfaction. Activity totals alone cannot establish parity with Khan Academy Kids or any other provider.

**Immediate next gate:** Finish review of the draft Grade 2 Tens & Ones Explorer in PR #15, including qualified Arabic review, curriculum suitability and human device testing, before broadening release scope.

## Foundation Release 1: auditable Grade 2 English (in progress)

- **Automated manifest:** `learning-quality-manifest.json` documents 5 modules, 20 skills (listen, speak, read, write) and two -ing/-and word families with 14 stages. Each entry holds its reference source, exact lesson outcome, limitations and explicit reviewer status.
- **Drift prevention:** `scripts/check-learning-quality-manifest.cjs` checks that the same module IDs, titles, weeks, objectives, word families, letter patterns, child-data protections and stable progress key are in the actual app.
- **CI integration:** `.github/workflows/validate.yml` runs the script on changes, making unexpected deletion or mismatched curricula visible to developers. **Passing code tests does not approve the education.**
- **Next real-world review:** ask an English Grade 2 teacher to evaluate all five modules, a qualified Arabic educator to review each relevant Arabic line, and an adult tester to check phonics playback, iPhone navigation and VoiceOver.
- **Readiness evidence:** for a draft activity, record `Accept / Revise / Hold`, exact module and skill ID, source PDF page, correction, tester role and date, and the reason for the decision. Keep personal reviewer/child data private.

## Foundation Release 2: one complete, approved-to-pilot lesson

Select one small Grade 2 lesson (likely Module 2 word families) and improve depth **without increasing counted questions**:
1. Age-appropriate learning goal for child and adult in clear English plus educator-reviewed Arabic.
2. Short worked picture/sound model; an adult can explain what to notice.
3. Guided try, optional hint, friendly retry, meaningful explanation.
4. Independent, non-punitive practice; children may replay at their own pace.
5. Real-world example and end-of-lesson reflection, without calling participation mastery.
6. Inclusive layout, RTL, VoiceOver and normal/slow sound review on actual devices.
7. Versioned review outcome and regression tests; respect children's privacy.

Compare experience quality with national and international learning platforms on these **dimensions**, not self-declared equivalence or raw question counts: sequencing, explanations, accessibility, bilingual accuracy, progress meaning, speech intelligibility, independence, reliability, safety, and offline/device experience.

## Foundation Release 3: repeatable Grade 1–6 production method

Once one lesson is reviewed and demonstrated to work:
- Create reusable teaching components (goal → model → try → correction → apply → review), activity metadata and accessible navigation.
- Extend module-by-module to selected subjects; prioritize foundational gaps and actual Qatar school requirements, not publishing a nominal grade's worth of superficial questions.
- Use a parent dashboard explaining **practised**, **needs support** and **not yet reviewed** separately; never present an algorithm's completion count as certified mastery.
- Keep an evidence bundle of curriculum mapping, activity lists, safety/privacy practices, QA results, accessibility review and teacher feedback. Make this available **if requested** by an official evaluator.
