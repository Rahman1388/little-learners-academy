🇶🇦 Qatar curriculum and national values

Qatar MOEHE publishes a curriculum framework and subject standards, and its private-school policy treats Arabic, Islamic Studies and Qatar History as compulsory subjects for applicable grades and groups. Rules directed at schools do not, by themselves, mean that this independent practice app has been licensed or certified.

✅ Our release-quality gates

🧪 Run Grade 1 Automated Quality Checks • فحص جودة الصف الأول

These six automatic checks run inside your browser using the same question bank as the learning app. A passing result checks data structure and selected answer computations only; it does not establish curriculum or language certification.

Each new subject lesson or game should pass these checkpoints before we describe it as reviewed or classroom-ready. The list is a development policy; it does not mean all checks have passed.

  1. 1. Learning objective Specify subject, actual grade/year, precise learning goal, source/framework, correct answer, explanation, and age suitability.
  2. 2. Qatar content & language Verify Arabic, Islamic Studies and Qatar History against applicable source materials with qualified subject and language reviewers. Keep unreviewed lessons labelled as pilots.
  3. 3. Audio quality Confirm every recording's pronunciation, clarity, pace, dialect suitability and correct speaker description on iPhone and Android. Hold ambiguous or missing recordings rather than substitute a misleading voice.
  4. 4. Inclusion & accessibility Test large touch controls, keyboard operation, assistive technology, Arabic right-to-left text, no-audio alternatives, contrast, 200% zoom and reduced-motion preferences.
  5. 5. Child privacy and legal review Minimize personal data, document local storage and external hosting, check Qatar Personal Data Privacy Protection Law obligations including Article 17, and assess consent/data-rights procedures before any future accounts or analytics.
  6. 6. Learning and family experience Ensure calm feedback, no manipulative rewards or unwanted ads, brief sessions, parent-friendly reports, no deceptive score claims, and meaningful offline practice suggestions.
  7. 7. Practical testing and sign-off Test mobile/tablet/desktop links, broken images and audio, refresh/back navigation and printing; record corrections and obtain parent, teacher and specialist feedback before broader release.

Full Grades 1–6 curriculum coverage, MOEHE objective-level mapping, all Arabic-language and religious-content approval, full accessibility compliance, universal audio clarity and legal compliance certification.

The number of mini-questions is not the same as the number of complete lessons, unique standards, or evidence of learning outcomes. Parents should treat progress counters as practice history, not test grades.

👨‍👩‍👧 What families should ask before trusting an app

  • Does the activity match what my child is actually studying this week?
  • Can my child understand instructions in English or Arabic without being misled by unclear audio?
  • Can they retry mistakes gently and learn why an answer is right?
  • Can I review progress and remove locally saved information?
  • Is the content age-appropriate, culturally respectful and free from distracting commercial pressure?
  • Are lessons clearly labelled as pilots when they have not had independent subject review?

For school use, ask a qualified teacher to compare specific activities with the school's learning objectives. We welcome structured expert review, but this site does not currently have a public feedback submission or user account system.
