Volunteer Educator Review Kit دليل المراجعة التعليمية التطوعية

Start with a short, respectful, no-cost review of a few supplementary activities. Use this guide to invite qualified educators, collect actionable corrections, and track what still needs checking.

This is an internal quality process, not school endorsement, ministry approval, employment, certification or a request for confidential student data.

1. Who do we need first?

📘 Primary classroom teacher

Review grade suitability, question accuracy, helpful explanations and clarity of directions. Start with 5–10 activities rather than the entire app.

🗣️ Native Arabic-speaking teacher

Review natural Modern Standard Arabic, accurate mathematical terms, Arabic right-to-left display and whether bilingual prompts accidentally reveal English answers.

🔬 Subject specialists, later

Seek mathematics, English literacy, Science, Islamic Studies and Qatar History expertise as those subject materials are prepared. Religious and national-history content should remain specialist-review-locked until checked.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent accessibility feedback, later

With appropriate permission and safeguards, test navigation, readability, visual presentation and child-friendly feedback. Do not collect child names, photos, contact details or identifiable reports.

2. An invitation you can send to a teacher

Assalamu Alaikum, I am developing Little Learners Academy, an independent, free English–Arabic supplementary learning website for primary-age children in Qatar. It is an early educational pilot, not an approved school curriculum. Would you be willing to review 5–10 short activities in your teaching subject, voluntarily, and point out any inaccurate answers, unsuitable grade levels, unclear explanations or language issues? The first review should take about 15–20 minutes, depending on the activities. There is no expectation to endorse the project or represent your school. We will not mention your name or school without permission, and we do not need any student's personal information. Thank you for considering it.

If you contact educators through a school, follow that school's permission rules. Do not suggest their personal review is an official institutional endorsement. Voluntary review must involve genuine consent, without pressure or unrealistic unpaid commitments.

4. How we track and act on feedback

  1. Prepare: Share only a relevant public activity link, its question ID, proposed learning objective and answer key.
  2. Check credentials: Privately confirm teaching subject, relevant training and experience before relying on an expert review. Avoid publicly posting identity documents.
  3. Review a small batch: Begin with 5–10 questions so the request is manageable and the reviewer can give thoughtful feedback.
  4. Log the finding: Record question ID, reviewer role, issue, requested revision and date. Do not upload student information.
  5. Revise: Correct verified issues and preserve saved progress IDs; if the answer changes materially, explicitly consider whether existing completions should be revalidated.
  6. Recheck: Have the reviewer confirm the correction where possible. Keep unreviewed content clearly labelled pilot.
  7. Plan a family usability test: Only once content is safe and applicable regulatory requirements and permissions have been clarified.

Important boundary: Educator feedback is not a ministry licence or school accreditation. Any Qatar educational-services, commercial-registration or child-data obligations need separate official clarification and/or qualified legal advice.
