# Academy brand name review — October 2026

**Status:** candidate evaluation only; no rebrand approved and no legal trademark clearance established.

## User-preferred proposed name
- English: **Luma Learn Academy**
- Suggested modern Arabic rendering for native-speaker validation: **أكاديمية لوما للتعلّم** (Arabic name transliteration + "Academy for Learning"). Do not present this as certified or as a Ministry-approved translation.
- Arabic transliteration of the English name could alternatively be **أكاديمية لوما ليرن**. Ask an Arabic education professional before formal publishing.
- Existing project remains **Little Learners Academy**, GitHub `Rahman1388/little-learners-academy`. Do not alter public site, repository URL, PWA identifiers, store labels or branding without explicit owner approval and legal screening.

## Public web-search findings (20 October 2026)
1. **Luma Learn** operates an education platform for learners, teachers and parents, describing a free 24/7 WhatsApp tutor, accessible at https://www.lumalearn.ai/ . This is a **substantial conceptual/name overlap** with our educational audience.
2. **Luma / لوما** is in use by a bilingual Arabic/English coding and robotics academy at https://www.lumacademy.org/ . Additional similarity exists in the Arabic-language education context.
3. Several other products use **Luma** for language learning (Google Play) and word games; standalone `Luma` is not distinctive enough to presume unique search placement.

These are *public name-collision signals*, **not an official trademark determination**. Lack of an exact search hit would also not prove the name is legally available.

## Recommendation
**Pause adoption of "Luma Learn Academy" as an official app name.** Keep the existing GitHub identity, progress-storage keys, public homepage and review branches unchanged. Compare more distinctive, easily pronounced bilingual names before investing in icons, domain purchases, social accounts or app-store paperwork.

## Name-selection checklist
- Distinctive in Qatar and internationally in educational services, including potential transliteration collisions in Arabic.
- Simple for children to pronounce, easy to search and recall.
- Arabic orthography and cultural suitability verified by a fluent specialist.
- Check Qatar Ministry of Commerce and Industry trademark records plus relevant international intellectual property resources with a qualified local professional.
- Search Apple App Store and Google Play by *exact name and similar variants* for both languages; search domains/social handles separately.
- Consider later grades, teaching tools, educational news and other future services, rather than overly narrow Grade 1 identity.
- Only rebrand after owner selection; preserve existing user progress and URLs with migration/redirect plans.

## Potential brainstorming directions — UNCHECKED
- A newly coined bilingual word or two-word combination rather than "Luma Learn".
- English/Arabic themes such as discovery, light, curiosity and growth, with original pronunciation-friendly spelling.
- These are creative directions, not claims that names, domains or trademarks are free.

## Immediate next development focus
Keep improving the working Grade 2 mobile review flow and the GitHub-only adult School Updates module independently of final branding. Existing review gates, including teacher sign-off and iPhone Safari/VoiceOver, remain pending.
