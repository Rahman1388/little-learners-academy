# Little Learners Academy — Qatar & international curriculum evidence review
**Evidence checkpoint: 4 November 2026 | Status: independent adult-research draft**

## Purpose and critical limitations

This is a research-and-review planning tool, **not** Qatar Ministry endorsement, approved learning outcomes, a substitute for schools, a grade-equivalence table, child testing, voice certification or a promise of educational impact. The general Qatar National Curriculum Framework available publicly at the time of review is an **older national framework** linked to the 2011–2016 strategy; check current subject-level standards and the actual Qatar school textbook before certifying individual competencies. Qatar Ministry Early Learning identifies **Grades 1–2 as Foundation Stage**, with distinct Arabic, English, mathematics, science and Islamic Studies teaching approaches. The publicly available curriculum overview alone does **not** prove that each app lesson is in the right grade or term.

Existing repository `main` still contains **475 audited distinct practice multiple-choice items across Grades 1–6**, separately from six guided writing prompts, as of the November 3 milestone. A pilot is not a complete curriculum. Draft Visual Learning Garden PR #28 adds **six concept webs, 24 concept leaves and six reasoning checks** in an independent branch. Their counts should never inflate the existing audited public inventory until deliberately audited and released.

## Official Qatar priorities — what they mean for software design

1. **Language, numeracy, inquiry, inclusion and cultural relevance.** The Qatar National Curriculum General Framework says curriculum review must consider goals, values, principles, coherence, engagement and cross-curricular competencies; its scope is broad and not proof of any exact grade-level match.
2. **Foundation practice rather than passive content.** MOEHE's Early Learning page identifies Grades 1–2 as the foundation stage. Prioritize playful but purposeful early language/numeracy examples, adult-guided conversation and opportunities for teachers to add context.
3. **Purposeful e-learning.** MOEHE describes interaction, creativity, microlearning, digital competence, adaptive learning and motivation as relevant directions. The app must respond by improving explanation, feedback and accessibility—not by merely adding badges or overstating gamification's impact.
4. **Current strategic context.** MOEHE's Education Strategy 2024–2030 promotes educational quality and learning motivation; an August 2026 ministry interview stresses future-ready skills, national identity and Islamic values. These are directional goals, not a legal app endorsement.

## Development priorities, sequenced

**Priority A — Standards crosswalk + evidence quality (this branch).** Every proposed module must record: its original item ID; stated learning intention; Qatar subject and grade band with **explicitly unverified** grade-specific status until matched to official learning outcomes; at least one credible international anchor; language-review status; scientific/maths factual assessment; children's accessibility; source URL; author/reviewer role and audit date. The JSON register in this folder is a model. Add exact grade outcome identifiers only after independently inspecting current authorized standards.

**Priority B — Language integrity and native-speaker review.** Keep English and Arabic visible together. Review each Arabic written form for formal Modern Standard Arabic appropriateness and grade readability, each English grapheme–phoneme correspondence by a qualified phonics educator, and recordings for stress, sound quality, isolated phonemes vs repeated whole words and realistic pace. Existing candidate English adult-synthetic voices and male Arabic fallback must not be mislabelled as child native voices; retain the manual C/D bus selections. The new Visual Learning Garden remains deliberately silent until the educational and audio reviews are completed.

**Priority C — Child development and more meaningful teaching.** Make the child **observe a worked example → manipulate an accurate model → explain a connection → apply the idea → receive specific non-punitive feedback → practise retrieval later**. No timed pressure, endless reward loop, public rankings, inappropriate age demands or false 'mastery' badges. Use school-linked terms/contexts only when appropriate and reviewed.

**Priority D — Future-ready technology without risky shortcuts.** Plan adaptive pathways driven by *on-device completed skill IDs* and adult-selected learning goals; do not introduce individual child profiling or cloud AI chat as an early feature. UNICEF Guidance on AI and Children version 3.0 (December 2025) emphasizes safety, privacy, fairness, explanation and inclusion. Before considering a future AI assistant, require human-approved content, closed domain knowledge, disclosure, input restrictions, no unsolicited model chat, parental oversight, privacy/security assessment, and reliable child-facing fallback. Human adults—not an AI model—must determine actual educational correctness and appropriateness.

## Acceptance gate for every new lesson (independent of inflated activity counts)

For an item to be eligible for live release:
- [ ] Learning intention and age/grade band documented; exact Qatar standard **verified** or honestly labelled supplementary.
- [ ] Original content and copyright permission recorded; no copyrighted lesson audio/books copied.
- [ ] International comparison documented as similarity or independently approved mapping—not declared 'certified' automatically.
- [ ] Mathematics/science answer and visual representations independently reviewed; real examples safe.
- [ ] Native Arabic linguistic check performed; language support not confused with full Arabic language curriculum.
- [ ] Any phonics audio actually listened to by qualified reviewers; no synthetic voice labelled 'child voice'.
- [ ] Real device: iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, large touch targets, keyboard and screen reader, offline behavior.
- [ ] No account, child name, recording, unnecessary data, manipulative engagement or automatic AI conversation.
- [ ] Parent-facing evidence of learning must separate **practised**, **understood with help**, and **independently demonstrated**; do not equate a correct MCQ with mastery.
- [ ] Owner approves merging/stores after the full audit; do not imply MOEHE endorsement or school approval.

## Review cadence that does not depend on local teachers being available

1. Prepare a **sample evidence packet** with six new learning maps, grade/subject objectives, source links, 10–15 phonics clips where relevant, and a list of 5–10 specific questions, all without student records.
2. Contact possible institutional/referral reviewers; **do not wait** to improve code-only quality and accessibility checks, but do not falsely state human accreditation.
3. Record a disposition for each error: `unreviewed / revise / accepted by independent reviewer with role, date and supporting evidence`.
4. Re-test on iOS and Android; only then decide, with the owner, whether to merge the relevant draft modules.
5. Repeat the evidence cycle per subject and grade before scaling toward the 600-lesson / 2,400-activity long-term goal.

This document and the machine-readable register protect rigor; their creation does **not** confer certification, complete coverage, or specific learning gains.
