What should SEBT and educators check?

  1. iPhone and tablet: screens open correctly, buttons are easy to touch, no sideways scrolling, Play/Pause works only when chosen.
  2. Bilingual quality: directions, captions and error explanations make sense in English and Arabic; right-to-left layout and math notations remain clear.
  3. Learning effectiveness: children understand that four groups of ten and three ones make 43, not simply that blue and yellow objects look attractive.
  4. Accessibility: headings, controls, feedback and visual descriptions work with VoiceOver and Android TalkBack; colors are backed up by words and numbers.
  5. Privacy and copyright: no learner accounts, voice recording, trackers or unlicensed third-party video or branded mind-map artwork.

Review status: automated draft tests passed before publishing these review snapshots; this does not replace manual review of these public copies, child comprehension testing or teacher approval.
