Release gates — no shortcuts

  1. Verify an exact, current Qatar grade-level textbook/standard for each item, or label it supplementary without grade-equivalency claims.
  2. Have a qualified native Arabic educator review every instruction, translation and language-learning example.
  3. Have a qualified English phonics reviewer listen to every relevant proposed audio candidate before calling it native-quality.
  4. Obtain independent review of factual accuracy, child developmental level and learning progression across Grades 1–6.
  5. Check real iPhone Safari and Android Chrome, accessibility/assistive technology, offline behavior and parent-child comprehension.
  6. Confirm security, child privacy, copyright permissions and appropriate age-sensitive technology choices.
  7. Secure explicit project-owner review before merging into the live app or any app-store publication.

Reviewers should receive: this page, the six-map demo, a short item-by-item checklist and a clear request for specific errors. No real child names, school records, voice recordings or personal contact details from children should be sent.

The detailed machine-readable register and automated checks are maintained in the feature branch. A passing test protects source traceability and honest status only; it cannot certify pedagogy, accessibility, learning gains or accreditation.
