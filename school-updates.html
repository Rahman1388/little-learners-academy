How we protect trust • معايير الموثوقية

Each entry identifies its publisher and provides a link to the source. Dated research keeps its publication date. Directory links are labelled as references—not breaking news. There are no push alerts, government affiliations, ads, accounts, student data, location collection or notifications from this page. A scheduled GitHub workflow may retrieve QNA's public RSS on the server after release; it does not visit the publisher from children's devices.

Parents should confirm school-specific messages and examination dates with their own school. International research explains educational trends but does not establish Qatar school rules. This directory will be improved through periodic adult editorial review, with changes checked before they are displayed.

Little Learners Academy is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Qatar Ministry of Education, Hukoomi, UNESCO or OECD.
