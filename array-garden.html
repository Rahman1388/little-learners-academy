👨‍👩‍👧 For parents and teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

The visual arrays introduce equal groups, repeated addition and multiplication. The first missions use small groups; later missions explore arrays up to 5 rows and 5 columns. This is a supplementary model overlapping England's Year 2 arrays and US Grade 2 equal-group concepts; Grades 2–3 should not be treated as equivalent across school systems.

Completion means the child eventually selected all twelve correct totals, possibly after hints. It does not certify independent mathematical mastery. The Arabic support text is a draft pending review. No student account, camera, microphone, new voices or ads are used.
