For parents and educators • للأهل والمعلمين

These questions introduce simple science, observation, habitats and care for nature. They are supplementary activities, not an official Qatar curriculum or a mastery test. Arabic wording and scientific content await teacher review.

No child names, microphone, camera, voice recording, advertisements, analytics or third-party requests are used. Only completed mission IDs are saved on this device. This first version intentionally has no spoken phonics or automatic sound because English and Arabic recordings still require qualified pronunciation review.
