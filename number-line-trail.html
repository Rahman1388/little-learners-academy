👨‍👩‍👧 Family Learning Guide • دليل الأسرة

Start with the yellow number. For addition, move one place right for each jump. For subtraction, move one place left. Ask the child to predict the answer before pressing Show one jump. If the answer is wrong, encourage another try without a timer.

ابدأ من العدد الأصفر. في الجمع تحرّك إلى اليمين، وفي الطرح تحرّك إلى اليسار. شجّع الطفل على التوقع أولاً ثم التحقق خطوة بخطوة.

This is supplementary Grade 1 number-sense practice, not an official school assessment or a complete curriculum. Results are saved locally on this device. The game adds no account, microphone, audio recordings or tracking, and awards at most one completion star per device.
