This quest is a review of 12 existing questions from the 54-question Grade 3 mathematics, English reading and science pilots. It does not add 12 unique syllabus questions. Hints encourage reasoning; the three badges simply mark completed review questions, not mastery.

This is independent, parent-guided supplementary practice, not a complete or officially approved Grade 3 or Qatar curriculum. Answers are text-based and do not assess speech. Progress is stored on this device only. No ads, student accounts or microphones.
