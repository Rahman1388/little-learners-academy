# Arabic curriculum and pronunciation research plan

Status: **research references and potential review contacts only**, updated 2026-10-12.
This file does not confer a license to copy third-party lessons, sound recordings, APK assets, textbooks or images into the app.

## Best reference order

1. **Qatar Ministry of Education and Higher Education** — national curriculum guidance, learning resources, and compulsory Arabic, Islamic Studies and Qatar History policy.
   - https://www.edu.gov.qa/ar/Content/CurriculumSection
   - https://www.edu.gov.qa/ar/Content/LearningResources
   - https://www.edu.gov.qa/ar/News/Details/1621800
   - https://maktabaty.edu.gov.qa/ar/pages/default.aspx
   - An example *older* first-grade planning document: https://privateschoolssearch.edu.gov.qa/Documents/Highlights/%D8%AC%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%B1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A.pdf
   - Reference official scope and sequence; verify current requirements with an active Grade 1 teacher. Do not claim MOEHE certification.
2. **Nahla wa Nahil** — Arabic children's literacy application (ages 4–11), teacher-developed reading comprehension platform. Use as a *design/teaching benchmark only*, not a source of copied UI, stories or APK audio.
   - https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.almanhal.nahlawanahil
3. **Al Jazeera Learning Arabic** — examples of beginner Arabic language learning and text presentation. Much material is not written for six-year-olds and should be adapted by an expert; reuse only with explicit permission.
   - https://learning.aljazeera.net/ar/lessons/level/beginner
4. **Mozilla Common Voice Arabic speech datasets** — possible research/evaluation resource with CC0 licensing, but the dataset's own usage conditions prohibit identifying speakers and re-hosting/re-sharing the dataset. The material is mostly sentence speech, not independently certified phonics. Do not import or publish clips without checking dataset terms and content suitability.
   - https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmu5wom3z00ceo107guxtg4xa
   - https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmrv0fgp00022nu077cdkkcny (female subset)
   - https://mozilladatacollective.com/datasets/cmrv0f62m001wnu077iwrjbbo (male subset)

## Recommended human-review workflow

- Recruit one **qualified native Arabic early-primary teacher** familiar with Qatar Grade 1 and one **native Arabic speaker with audio/phonetics competence**, if possible.
- Give reviewers the written word, diacritics if appropriate, intended Modern Standard Arabic pronunciation, pronunciation audio (male and female **adult-style**, never presented as actual children), and child's learning objective.
- Ask them independently to mark **correct word**, **clear articulation**, **suitable pace**, **age suitability**, **regional appropriateness**, **recording quality**, and **approved/not approved**, including detailed notes.
- Request new **consented, original recordings** when existing clips fail; get written permission covering public app distribution, revisions, and future use. Do not scrape or extract from APKs or copy proprietary lessons.
- Preserve the original rejected recordings in the adult-only QA tool for comparison; do not put failed clips back in children's activities.
- Record speaker type, language, phonetic transcription/diacritics, reviewer name or professional role (with consent), date, version, approved scope, and license alongside assets.

## App-building rule

Do not install APKs from untrusted sources or extract third-party app assets, recordings or code. Use third-party websites as references and request explicit permission or use independently created materials. A dataset's open license does not guarantee age suitability or correctness of every pronunciation.
