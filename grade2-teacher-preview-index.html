Safeguards for reviewers

Use adult-only test examples. Do not enter a child's name, address, school, photograph, voice recording or other identifying information. The feedback helper cannot automatically anonymize a person's writing. Do not share a completed review without checking its contents.

Words are read aloud by an adult, not by built-in recorded child voices; the app does not assess oral pronunciation or fluency. Practised stages are not mastery scores. Educator review of the content and Arabic wording, and real-iPhone Safari/VoiceOver checks, are still pending.

This package is for controlled local or separately approved staging use. It does not create a public preview URL, replace the existing public app, submit feedback to the school or Ministry, or certify curriculum alignment.
