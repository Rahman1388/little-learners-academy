👨‍👩‍👧 For parents and teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

These 12 original, hands-on sharing tasks support the concepts of equal parts, halves, thirds and fourths commonly introduced in Grade 2 mathematics (such as US 2.G.A.3). Equal pieces of the same whole represent a fraction. Encourage children to describe what they shaded and to compare pieces of the same-sized whole.

Supplementary Grade 2 readiness pilot, not a complete or certified Qatar curriculum. This uses existing on-device controls only—no new voice recordings, microphones, accounts, ads or trackers. Completing a round doesn't add duplicate stars or change Grade 1 progress.
