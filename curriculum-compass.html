    Arabic, Islamic Studies and Qatar History are important compulsory areas under applicable Qatar private-school policy. Formal national-content lessons are review-locked until approved against appropriate Qatar source materials and checked by qualified specialists. The Arabic translation on a maths question does not make it an Arabic-curriculum lesson.

    🏫 How a parent can compare this with schoolwork

    • Choose the child's actual grade and school system above; British Year and American Grade labels are not automatically interchangeable.
    • Compare a school textbook chapter or weekly learning objective to the topic names shown here—not just the activity count.
    • Try one relevant game together and ask the child to explain the answer. A completed activity records practice, not an official assessment.
    • For precise mapping, use the school’s published scheme of work or textbook contents. Do not share student names, grades/reports with identity details, or children's personal records.
