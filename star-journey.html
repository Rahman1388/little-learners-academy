🎧 Listen & Repeat Corner ركن الاستماع والتكرار

Pick an English voice style, tap a picture, then repeat the whole word three times. اختر الصوت الإنجليزي، واضغط على الصورة، ثم كرر الكلمة ثلاث مرات.

Tap a picture to hear its English word three times.

These are adult synthetic speaker styles—not real children. They replay existing whole-word recordings; isolated phonemes and formal pronunciation instruction still require teacher review. Arabic meanings are shown for support.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Teacher Corner ركن الأهل والمعلمين

Encourage a short, enjoyable session. Try one game, listen to two words, and celebrate the effort—not just the stars. All progress is saved on this device, with no account or tracking.

This is supplementary practice, not a Qatar curriculum certification or a substitute for qualified instruction. The app does not assess speech or microphone pronunciation.
