👨‍👩‍👧 For parents • للأهل والمعلمين

Choose by what the child is ready to learn, not simply their age. Grade 1 is the broadest playable foundation; Grades 2–4 offer a small selection of practice questions, with Grade 4 offering maths, English reading and science pilots. Formal Arabic, Islamic Studies and Qatar-specific curriculum content need qualified specialist review before release.

No account, student name, ads, microphone or personal information is needed to choose a level. Existing on-device progress and approved audio stay unchanged.
