These 151 original mini-challenges introduce supplementary learning objectives from early primary maths, English literacy, science, life skills and visual patterns. They are organised around themes overlapping British KS1 and US Grade 1; actual classroom sequence and difficulty vary. They are not an official Qatar Grade 1 curriculum, school syllabus, or assessment. Arabic is supportive text, not an approved Arabic-language curriculum module. For formal pronunciation instruction, qualified educator review remains needed.

Encourage short sessions and conversation about the answers. Every challenge is free and works without an account, microphone, advertising or external APIs.
