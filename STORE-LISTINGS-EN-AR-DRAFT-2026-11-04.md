## English app description
Welcome to Little Learners Academy, a colourful learning space for curious primary-school children and their families.

Explore early reading, mathematics, science, hands-on visual puzzles and creative activities. Start with Grade 1 foundations and try selected supplementary practice for Grades 2–6.

Children can choose short adventures, practise with helpful hints, revisit mistakes, and share discoveries with a parent or teacher. English and Arabic appear together in many activities, with selected audio listening practice. A calm pace and natural stopping points help keep learning comfortable.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPLORE:
- Visual number games, early maths and shape activities.
- Reading, words, pictures and selected phonics demonstrations.
- Science discoveries, matching, stories and creative tasks.
- Simple learning routes, friendly feedback and family conversation ideas.
- Progress on the device without a child account.

Little Learners Academy is an independent supplementary educational app. It is not an official curriculum, school assessment, or Ministry of Education endorsement. Individual lessons and Arabic translations are reviewed progressively.

IMPORTANT: Availability, offline support and voice functionality vary by the final iOS/Android version. Confirm all features in the signed app before using this description publicly.
