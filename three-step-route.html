This is a suggested practice route, not an assessment. Tap “I tried this” after a child has attempted an activity. A check mark means tried, not mastered. Grade 1 includes broader foundational practice; Grades 2–6 link to limited subject pilots requiring teacher and Arabic-language review, not official Qatar curricula.

Choosing a grade and marking steps stores only simple checkmarks on this device; there is no account, child name, recording, timer or score upload. You may switch grades at any time. The current approved voice settings are untouched.
