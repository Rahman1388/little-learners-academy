👨‍👩‍👧 Note for parents and teachers

Start with Grade 1 counting and place value. Use real objects, pencils and paper as well as the questions on this screen. Encourage children to explain their thinking. Mistakes are opportunities to learn.

The Arabic text provides supporting directions only and requires language review. English synthetic phonics and Arabic pronunciation are not used in this pilot. No camera, microphone, accounts, ads or child personal details are requested. Completion records stay only on this device.
