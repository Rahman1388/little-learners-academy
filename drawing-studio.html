For parents and teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

This is a creative supplementary activity for early-primary fine-motor practice, visual expression, shape recognition and colours. It is not a graded drawing assessment or officially approved Qatar curriculum unit. No microphone, accounts, trackers, or new audio recordings are required. Artwork stays on the device; the app does not upload children’s drawings.
