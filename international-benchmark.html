🗺️ Our staged development plan

🌱 Foundation: Grade 1 Improve number sense, early reading, practical science, listening clarity and parent guidance.
🌿 Next: Grade 2 Build distinct place-value, reading, time and money units after content review; not simply rename Grade 1 tasks.
🌳 Later: Grades 3–4 Introduce multiplication, fractions, comprehension and experiments with teacher-reviewed difficulty.
🌟 Finally: Grades 5–6 Extend to decimals, ratios, research and explanation, with age-appropriate accessibility and QA.
