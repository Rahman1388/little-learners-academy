Tens & Ones Explorer العشرات والآحاد

Today's learning goal: Build a two-digit number using groups of ten and single ones, then explain what each digit means.

Not a test or official school lesson. This is an original, supplementary Grade 2 teaching prototype. Arabic wording, age suitability and curriculum alignment require qualified review. No scores, accounts, recordings, timers or progress storage.

Step 1 of 4 • Watch a model

Look at tens and ones

A ten-stick stands for ten. Each small dot stands for one.

Look at the blue tens sticks and gold ones dots.

Look carefully. You can take as long as you need.

👨‍👩‍👧 A note for parents and teachers

Ask the child to touch or count one ten-stick as ten, then count the single dots. After the last practice question, encourage an explanation in their own words. Do not mark a child as having mastered place value just because they completed these screens.

This lesson does not connect to external services or save answers. The illustrated tens are schematic teaching models, not physical manipulatives. Please confirm developmental suitability, accessibility and draft bilingual labels with an educator before release.
