🧭 What the map does—and doesn't mean

Our activities overlap with broad early-primary topics used in British KS1 and US Grade 1. This is a supplementary practice map, not a verified standards crosswalk or evidence of full mastery.

Still needing curriculum and specialist review:
  • Explicit progression for phoneme-level decoding, spelling and reading fluency
  • Teacher-reviewed Arabic literacy, Qatar History and Islamic Studies
  • Broader writing, extended comprehension, measurement and place-value assessments
  • Pronunciation checks by qualified English and Arabic educators

Completing an activity means a correct answer was recorded at least once, not that a child has independently mastered the skill. Parents can ask their child to explain an answer, practise with real objects, and revisit difficult questions.
