🌟 Little Learners on Your Phone أكاديمية المتعلمين الصغار على هاتفك

Use the current learning app from your iPhone or Android home screen—no laptop or developer account needed just to use this version.

يمكنك فتح التطبيق من الشاشة الرئيسية على آيفون أو أندرويد.

Choose the steps for your phone below.

🍎 iPhone / iPad • آيفون / آيباد

Recommended for families in Qatar. These instructions install the website to your Home Screen; this is not yet an Apple App Store download.

1

Open Little Learners Academy in Safari on your iPhone. If you're currently viewing this inside another app, use its menu to open in Safari.

2

Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). Depending on your iOS version, you may need to use the Safari page menu to find Share.

3

Select Add to Home Screen. If offered, select Open as Web App.

4

Tap Add. Open the new Little Learners icon from your Home Screen!

افتح الموقع في سفاري، ثم المشاركة ← إضافة إلى الشاشة الرئيسية ← إضافة.

🤖 Android • أندرويد

1

Open Little Learners Academy in Chrome on your Android device.

2

Open Chrome's menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen (wording varies).

3

Confirm and open the icon on your Home Screen.

🏬 App Store & Google Play: coming later

We are preparing a separate signed, tested mobile application for the official stores. The website installed from your browser is not an approved store listing, so there are no App Store or Google Play download links yet.

Store release requires developer accounts, real device testing, child-privacy review, accurate screenshots and approval from each store. We will not ask you to download an unofficial IPA or APK.
