👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & teachers • للأهل والمعلمين

This is a Grade 1 extension / Grade 2 bridge, introducing equal-size jumps and repeated addition. Children first watch one jump at a time, then explain their answer. Try using two blocks, five fingers or ten counters to model each jump. The last two missions gently introduce jumping backwards.

Supplementary practice only; school sequence and exact national standard vary. Draft Arabic prompts have not received specialist review. No account, advertising, microphone, audio replacement or individual child profile is required. Progress is kept on this device. Completing the twelve missions awards one star at most.
