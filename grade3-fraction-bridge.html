👨‍👩‍👧 Parent and teacher notes • ملاحظات للمعلمين والأهل

This is an original, unapproved supplementary Grade 3 pilot, with 12 distinct in-session questions: four reading, four comparison and four equivalence exercises. The warm-up is a teaching interaction, not another scored question. These are not added to the production 475-question audit until reviewed and released.

Ask your learner to explain *why* two fractions match or differ. Fraction bars are equal-width wholes; pieces within each bar have equal sizes. Same-denominator comparisons are introduced before equivalent fractions.

Review required: Grade 3 maths educator; fluent Arabic educator for translations; real iPhone Safari, tablet, keyboard and screen-reader checks. This is not certified for Qatar's school curriculum and is not an official school grade.

Privacy and audio: No accounts, microphone, sound playback, adverts, analytics, API requests, third-party fonts, storage or recorded child information. It does not change existing stars or progress. No unreviewed voice is presented as an authentic child voice.
