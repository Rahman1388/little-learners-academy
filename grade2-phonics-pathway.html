Display accessibility: The Larger letters button changes the practice letters without recording preferences. Buttons show their selected state, and the page includes a skip link and optional Arabic stage names. These are draft aids pending iPhone VoiceOver and Arabic educator review.

Visual learning: The yellow, underlined word ending is an on-screen spelling clue. It does not assess what a learner hears or pronounces. A new optional “Next” button appears after each guided stage is practised; children can still choose stages freely.

Read the adult script aloud at a comfortable pace and let the learner listen without seeing your words. In blending, guide the learner through the starting sound and shared ending; when practising -ack, bl and sn are beginning consonant blends, while the -ain word train starts with the tr blend and the -ay word play begins with pl. The on-screen response checks spelling or recognition only; it does not assess oral blending, pronunciation, comprehension mastery or fluency.

Keep the child supported and do not rush. Some children will need easier examples or multisensory support; please flag unsuitable words for educator review. The Arabic heading and directions need native-language educator approval before wider release.
