This is an original 24-question Grade 2 English readiness pilot, not a full school syllabus or Qatar curriculum approval. English reading skills are supported by short Arabic question prompts, not independently reviewed Arabic literacy lessons. The four strands cover literal comprehension, straightforward inference, word meanings, and sentence-writing basics. Questions are designed for guided practice, not grading or reading-level diagnosis. Some children will need an adult to read the text together. No microphone, recordings, account, tracking or advertising is used; saved progress stays on this device.
